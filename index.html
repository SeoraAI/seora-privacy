Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 31, 2026

Seora AI ("we," "our," or "the app") is operated by Nice People Collective LLC. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Do Not Collect

Seora AI is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not require you to create an account or log in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technologies.
  • We do not collect analytics or usage data.

Data Stored on Your Device

Any information you enter into the app — such as shop settings, product details, or saved calculations — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device except when you use the AI SEO generator feature, as described below.

AI SEO Generator

When you use the AI-powered SEO listing generator, the product details you enter (such as product name, type, audience, and style) are sent to a secure server to generate your listing content. This data is:

  • Processed in real time to generate your SEO content.
  • Not stored on our servers after the response is delivered.
  • Not used to train any AI models.
  • Not shared with any third parties.

We use Anthropic's Claude AI through a secure Supabase Edge Function to process these requests. You can review Anthropic's privacy policy for additional details on their data handling practices.

Third-Party Services

Seora AI uses the following third-party service to power the AI SEO generator:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — for generating SEO listing content. Data is processed in real time and not retained.
  • Supabase — for securely routing API requests. No user data is stored.

Children's Privacy

Seora AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
